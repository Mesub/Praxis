# PRAXIS — The Mastery Operating System

> **Learning is a build system. The goal is the build target. The knowledge graph is the
> dependency DAG. What you already know is the cache.**
> **Forgetting is cache invalidation.**
>
> PRAXIS is the compiler, the scheduler, and the test suite — and it never lets you ship a build
> that didn't pass its tests.

---

## The refusal that defines the product

**There is no "mark as complete" button in PRAXIS.** Anywhere. It does not exist.

Nothing is recorded as learned unless the learner **produced evidence** — solved a never-seen
problem, explained it aloud, shipped something that runs, found the flaw, or **predicted the
real world and reality agreed**.

Everything else in this blueprint follows from that one refusal.

## The measure of success

Not signups. Not DAU. Not hours watched.

> **Did the learner become the thing they said they wanted to become — and can they prove it to
> a stranger?**

If PRAXIS cannot beat a good course on 90-day retention in a published trial, it does not
deserve to exist.
